# Bundled Audio Samples for Streamlit Demo

This directory contains 10 benchmark environmental audio clips (one per target class) extracted from the ESC-50 dataset for instant evaluation in the SoundVision AI application.

## Dataset Attribution & Licensing
- Dataset: ESC-50 (Dataset for Environmental Sound Classification)
- Author: Karol J. Piczak
- Reference: Piczak, K. J. (2015). ESC: Dataset for Environmental Sound Classification. Proceedings of the 23rd ACM International Conference on Multimedia.
